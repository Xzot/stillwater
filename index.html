Questions and answers

If you are stuck or something looks wrong, write to shchukavlad4@gmail.com. Mention your device model and iOS or iPadOS version if you can. We usually answer within two days.

How do I finish a board?

Every tile has to be joined to the network that starts at the spring, the round tile, and no pipe may point at nothing. When that is true, a wave of color rolls across the board. Any arrangement that follows the rule counts, not only one “correct” picture.

What are “Edges meet” boards?

They have a dashed frame. The edges are joined: a pipe that leaves on the right comes back on the left, and the top joins the bottom. In version 1.0 this was called Wrap.

What is free, and what does the Full Game open?

Dawn (150 boards) and today’s spring (one board a day) are free.

The Full Game is a one-time purchase that opens the sluice: Forest, Rain, Honeycomb, Ocean, Desert and Night (150 boards each), and open water, where you pick any size from 5×5 to 12×12 and can let the edges meet. There are no ads and no subscription. Family Sharing works.

If you bought the Full Game before version 1.1, you get Rain and Honeycomb too.

I bought the Full Game on another device

In the app, open the Journal (the link at the bottom of the river) and tap Restore Purchases. Use the same Apple Account you bought with.

How do days in a row work?

Solve today’s spring on the day itself and the run grows by one. If you miss a single day in a week, the run carries on. Days follow your device’s local date.

There is no sound

Stillwater follows the Ring/Silent switch, so it stays quiet when your iPhone or iPad is on silent. Also check Journal, then Quiet settings: music and sound effects have separate switches.

How do I add the widget?

Touch and hold the Home Screen, tap Edit, then Add Widget. Find Stillwater and choose Today’s spring.

For the Lock Screen, touch and hold it, tap Customize, choose Lock Screen, and add the widget from the list.

Can I get a refund?

Apple handles purchases, so refunds go through them: reportaproblem.apple.com.
