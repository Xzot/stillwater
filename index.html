Support

Stillwater is a quiet puzzle: tap tiles to turn them until water from the spring reaches every pipe and nothing leaks. There's no timer and no way to lose.

Need help? Email shchukavlad4@gmail.com. Tell us your iPhone model and iOS version if something looks wrong. We usually answer within two days.

How do I finish a board?

Every tile has to be joined to the network that starts at the spring (the round tile), and no pipe may point at an empty side or off the board. When that's true, a wave of color rolls across the board. Any arrangement that follows these rules counts, not just one "correct" picture.

What does Wrap mean?

On Wrap boards (marked with a dashed frame) the edges are connected: a pipe that leaves on the right comes back on the left, and top joins bottom.

What's free and what's in the Full Game?

Dawn (150 levels) and Daily Flow are free. The Full Game is a one-time purchase that unlocks Forest, Ocean, Desert and Night (150 levels each), Endless mode and Wrap in Endless. There are no ads and no subscription. Family Sharing is supported.

I bought the Full Game on another device

Open Settings in Stillwater and tap Restore Purchases, signed in with the same Apple Account.

How does the Daily Flow streak work?

Solve the day's puzzle on that day to add to your streak. If you miss one day in a week, the streak stays. Days follow your iPhone's local date.

There's no sound

Stillwater follows the Ring/Silent switch, so it stays quiet when your iPhone is on silent. Also check Settings in the app: music and sound effects have separate switches.

How do I add the widget?

Touch and hold the Home Screen, tap Edit, then Add Widget, find Stillwater and choose Daily Flow. On the Lock Screen: touch and hold, tap Customize, then Lock Screen, and add it from the widget list.

Can I ask for a refund?

Purchases are handled by Apple. You can request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.
