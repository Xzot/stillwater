Privacy Policy

Stillwater: Calm Pipe Puzzle is made by Vladyslav Shchuka (“we”). The short version: the app collects nothing.

What we collect

Nothing. We run no servers. The app has no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no tracking, and it has nothing of ours to connect to.

What stays on your iPhone

Your progress, boards in progress, statistics, days in a row and settings live on your iPhone and nowhere else. Deleting the app deletes them.

The widget

The widget shows your days in a row, whether today’s spring is done and how many boards you have finished. The app saves those numbers in storage on your iPhone that the app and the widget both can read. They never leave the device.

Purchases

The Full Game is sold by Apple through the App Store, so we never see your payment details. The app only asks Apple whether your Apple Account owns the Full Game. How Apple treats that is covered by Apple’s Privacy Policy.

Children

The app is rated 4+. It collects nothing from anyone, children included.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.
